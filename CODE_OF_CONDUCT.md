# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the LuckyBox community respectful, safe, and welcoming for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept feedback professionally.
- Focus discussion on the project and its users.
- Protect private information and credentials.
- Report security issues responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, credential theft, malicious code, spam, and publication of another person's private information are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

## Reporting

Use the repository's private security reporting channel when available. Do not publish exploitable vulnerabilities or secrets in public issues.
